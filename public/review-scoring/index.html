How we score games

Every score is an editorial judgment of the game as reviewed. The number is a quick guide to our overall experience; the review and verdict explain the reasons behind it.

ScoreWhat it means
9–10Exceptional. A standout experience with qualities that rise well above its shortcomings.
8–8.9Excellent. Strongly recommended, with only limited issues holding it back.
7–7.9Good. Enjoyable and worthwhile, though some notable flaws remain.
6–6.9Mixed. Clear strengths, but meaningful problems limit the overall experience.
4–5.9Weak. The problems outweigh the experience’s stronger parts.
0–3.9Poor. Serious problems make it difficult to recommend.

How to read a score

  • Scores run from 0 to 10. Decimals are allowed, and the exact score appears in the review verdict.
  • The score is not an average of categories or a reader poll. It summarizes the reviewer’s overall critical judgment.
  • Star icons on story cards are rounded to the nearest whole star for quick scanning; they do not replace the exact score.
  • Read the verdict and the full review for context. Two games with the same score can have different strengths and drawbacks.

Our reviews are independently written. Scores reflect the reviewer’s testing and critical judgment; they are not bought or set by publishers or sponsors. See our Terms of Service.
