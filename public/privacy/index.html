Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026 • Effective Date: September 21, 2026

AstroBitPlays ("we", "our", or "us"), available at https://astrobitplays.me, is an independent digital gaming publication providing video game journalism, news, comprehensive reviews, video embeds, and editorial guides.

This Privacy Policy describes how AstroBitPlays collects, uses, and protects your information when you access our website or utilize user accounts to save and organize articles.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection. We only collect information strictly necessary to provide our publication and account services:

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in using Google OAuth ("Continue with Google"), we receive your public profile information consisting of your email address, verified display name, and avatar image URL.
  • Email Authentication: If you choose email authentication, we collect your email address and an encrypted hash of your chosen password or one-time magic link token.
  • Reading List & Bookmarks: We store references to articles you choose to bookmark ("Save article") so that your saved library syncs across devices.
  • Anonymous Technical Metrics: Standard aggregate logs (such as page view counts, device types, and browser user-agents) to evaluate site performance and article popularity.

Google API Disclosure & Limited Use Compliance

AstroBitPlays uses Google OAuth solely for user identity authentication. We access only standard OpenID Connect scopes (email, profile, openid). AstroBitPlays' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We never sell your Google data or use it for advertising.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity and allow you to sign in securely.
  • To maintain and synchronize your saved article reading list.
  • To verify authorization permissions for editorial studio management.
  • To monitor and prevent spam, malicious traffic, or platform abuse.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data. Your information is never shared with third-party data brokers or marketing platforms. Data is only processed through trusted infrastructure providers:

  • Supabase: Our database and authentication infrastructure is hosted through Supabase (utilizing AWS data centers) with strict row-level security and TLS encryption.
  • Google Identity Services: Used to verify Google accounts upon sign-in.
  • Embedded Content: Articles may include interactive embeds (such as YouTube videos, X/Twitter posts, or Twitch streams). These third-party services may collect telemetry or cookies in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

4. Data Retention & Security

We maintain strict administrative and technical security measures to safeguard your personal data. Your account information is protected with industry-standard cryptographic hashing and role-based access policies. We retain your information only as long as your account remains active.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion (GDPR & CCPA)

You have full control over your personal data:

  • Access and Portability: You can review your saved articles and account information at any time.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You may request complete, irreversible deletion of your account and associated bookmarks by emailing us at karacostas.jim@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". We process all requests within 48 hours.
  • Revoking Google Access: You can revoke AstroBitPlays' access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account Security Settings.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

AstroBitPlays utilizes browser local storage and essential cookies strictly to maintain authentication sessions. We do not use intrusive third-party cross-site advertising trackers.
